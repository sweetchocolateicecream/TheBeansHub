The Origin Story

The Beans Hub started with a simple frustration.

Finding good Arabica coffee beans in Malaysia shouldn't be this hard. You either stumble across a roaster by luck, spend hours jumping between Instagram pages and Shopee listings, or just default to whatever's on the shelf at your nearest cafe supplier.

We thought there had to be a better way. So we built one.

The Beans Hub is a curated directory of Arabica coffee beans available in Malaysia — organised by taste, origin, roast level, processing method, and more. We don't sell the beans. We just help you find exactly what you're looking for, then point you straight to the roaster.

Whether you're chasing something chocolatey and smooth, or a bright fruity natural from Ethiopia — we've done the legwork so you don't have to.
