The Criteria

Four things we check before a roaster gets listed.

1
They roast in Malaysia
The Beans Hub is a Malaysian specialty coffee platform. All roasters on it must roast here.
2
They use specialty-grade green coffee
Commodity-grade coffee is not on the platform. Specialty grade (SCA 80+) only.
3
They publish a roast date
No roast date = no transparency. We don't list beans we can't tell you are fresh.

Criterion 4

They provide accurate tasting notes and origin information.

Why this matters

The Beans Hub is a discovery platform. People use it to find beans based on flavour profile and origin. If a roaster lists "good coffee" as a tasting note, that helps nobody. We need specific, accurate information — Ethiopian, washed, notes of jasmine and stone fruit — so the filter actually works for the person using it.

Vague product listings are a signal. If a roaster doesn't know what's in their bag, we're not listing it.

Why It Matters

The platform is only useful if what's on it is worth finding.

The honest reason

We are building a discovery platform. If someone searches for a natural-process Ethiopian and finds it easily on The Beans Hub, they come back. If they search and find low-quality listings with no real information, they don't. The curation standard is not elitist — it is just what makes the platform work for the person using it.

There are more Malaysian specialty roasters than most people know about. The good ones have plenty of space here.
