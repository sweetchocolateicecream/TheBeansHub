Rank 5

Nespresso. It's complicated.

Honest take

Nespresso is a vibe, not a coffee. It is convenient, consistent, and many people love it. It also produces more waste than any other method and locks you into proprietary pods. It is not specialty coffee. It is also fine. Both things are true.

If you own a Nespresso and you feel attacked: we said what we said. The coffee is still fine.

The Actual Point

The best brew method is the one you'll actually use.

Genuinely

This whole ranking is somewhat made up. The V60 produces better coffee than the French Press only if you use it consistently and with good beans. The worst outcome is not owning a Nespresso — it's owning a V60 that sits in a cupboard unused because the process is too much at 7am.

Use whatever gets you to a good cup on a Tuesday morning. That's the whole game.
