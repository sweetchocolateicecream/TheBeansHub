What We Learned

You already know more about coffee than you think you do.

The observation

The posts that got the most saves were the ones that named specific things — water temperature, roast dates, origin differences. The people reading this are not beginners looking for permission slips. You are curious people who want to understand what's actually happening in the cup. That's a very specific kind of person and there are more of you than you realise in Malaysia.

The "I just want coffee" crowd stopped following us around day 4. You are the ones still here.

What We Learned

The Malaysian specialty coffee scene is genuinely impressive.

The observation

Thirty days of talking about local roasters taught us that most people outside the specialty coffee bubble have no idea how good Malaysian-roasted coffee has gotten. The quality is there. The discoverability isn't. That's the whole reason The Beans Hub exists — and 30 days into building it, that problem feels more real and more solvable than when we started.

There are roasters in this country doing world-class work. Most people have just never heard of them.
