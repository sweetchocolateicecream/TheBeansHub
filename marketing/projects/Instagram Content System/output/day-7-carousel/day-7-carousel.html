Variable 01

The humidity in your kitchen.

The Detail

Coffee is hygroscopic — it drinks moisture straight out of the air. On a sticky Malaysian morning your grounds clump and water creeps through them slower, so the cup comes out heavier and muddier. On a dry day the same beans run faster and taste thinner. You brewed it the same way you always do, and the room still changed the result.

It's also why cafés re-dial their grind every single morning before they open. Same beans, new weather, new setting.

Variable 02

Water temperature.

The Detail

Coffee extracts differently at different temperatures. A few degrees off and you shift from bright and clean to flat and bitter. The sweet spot is around 88–96°C. Most people use just-boiled water at 100°C, which can scorch lighter roasts.

Let it sit 30 seconds after boiling. That's genuinely enough. You don't need a thermometer.

Variable 03

How long since you opened the bag.

The Detail

Beans start going stale the moment they hit oxygen. An opened bag loses noticeable flavour within 1–2 weeks, even if you seal it tight. Day 3 of an opened bag tastes different from day 14. Both cups are from the same bag.

This is why you buy 200–250g at a time. Not a 1kg bag. Not even close.

Variable 04

Your grinder matters more than your coffee machine.

The Detail

Even with the same grinder setting, the grind can vary slightly based on bean density, humidity, and how full the hopper is. That variation matters more than most people realise — a RM10,000 machine paired with a cheap grinder will consistently lose to a RM1,500 machine with a great one. The grinder controls how evenly water passes through your coffee. Uneven grind means uneven extraction, and that's what ends up as a flat, bitter, or sour cup.

Variable 05

Your own palate. It changes day to day.

The Detail

What you ate before, whether you've brushed your teeth, how much sleep you got, even stress — all of these affect how you taste. Coffee researchers call this "taster state". Most of us just call it "the coffee tastes weird today."

Sometimes the coffee is fine. You are just having a Monday.
