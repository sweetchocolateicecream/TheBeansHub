Pick 01 — Morning

The 8am focus cup.

What to look for
Washed Ethiopian or Kenyan · Light roast
Bright, clean, floral or citrus notes
High acidity wakes your palate up the same way a shower does. The brightness keeps you alert without the heavy body that makes you sluggish. Best brewed V60 or drip. Drink it while it's still warm — the flavour shifts as it cools and that's actually worth experiencing.

This is the cup that makes you feel like a functional adult before 9am.

Pick 02 — Afternoon

The 3pm pick-me-up.

What to look for
Colombian or Brazilian · Medium roast
Chocolatey, nutty, low-medium acidity
At 3pm your stomach has had food and you want something comforting, not sharp. A medium-roast Colombian or Brazilian gives you caramel, chocolate, nuts — heavier body without the brightness of a light roast. Aeropress works perfectly here. Quick to brew, reliable, less precious.

The 3pm coffee dilemma is real. This solves it without affecting your sleep. Mostly.

Pick 03 — Evening

The 6pm wind-down cup.

What to look for
Natural process · Any origin · Medium roast
Fruity, complex, lower caffeine than light roast
A naturally processed bean is something you sip slowly. The fruit character gives it enough complexity to drink without feeling like you're fuelling up. French press works well here — the heavy body and texture suit the slower pace of the evening. This one is about enjoyment, not productivity.

You are still going to feel it at midnight. Just so you know.
