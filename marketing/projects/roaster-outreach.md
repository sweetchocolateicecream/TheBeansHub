# Roaster Outreach — Founding Partner Pitch

*Built 18 Jun 2026. Use this morning. Send the 5 emails before you do anything else.*

---

## READ THIS FIRST — on pricing (the uncomfortable part)

The growth playbook says charge RM499/month with a guarantee of "50 referral clicks in 30 days or it's free." **Do not send that to a cold roaster yet.** Two problems:

1. **You can't honor that guarantee until you know your traffic.** If your site sends fewer than ~50 clicks/month to a given roaster, that guarantee auto-triggers and you work for free. Pull your real number from GA4 (Reports → Engagement → Events → filter `buy_click`, or Pages and screens for top landing pages) **before** you quote anyone a click guarantee.
2. **A cold RM499 invoice from a site they've barely heard of gets ignored.** The money is real, but the *first* email's job is to start a relationship and show them you're already sending them buyers — not to close a sale.

So the play is **two steps**:

- **Email 1 (today):** "You're featured on The Beans Hub, here's what I'm building, here's the data." Warm them. Get a reply or a call.
- **Email 2 / the call:** present the Founding Partner offer below.

That's it. Send 5 today. Follow up non-replies at day 3, day 7, day 14 (the playbook is right about follow-up — most sales happen there).

## The Founding Roaster Partner offer (use on the call, not cold)

> **The Beans Hub — Founding Roaster Partner**
> **RM199/month** — founding rate, locked for life. (Standard rate will be RM499 once traffic scales. First 5 partners only.)
>
> What you get:
> - Your full catalogue listed and filterable on thebeanshub.com — found by people *already searching* for specialty beans in Malaysia
> - A dedicated roastery spotlight page, SEO-optimised to rank for "[your brand] coffee beans"
> - One homepage featured-bean placement each month (highest-visibility spot on the site)
> - A monthly report: exactly how many clicks we sent to your store
> - Your beans included in the Beans Hub email newsletter
> - A "Beans Hub Founding Partner" badge for your own site and Instagram
>
> **Honest guarantee (one you can actually keep):** Stay 60 days. If you don't think it's worth it, cancel and I'll keep your spotlight live free for another month — no questions. *(Note: this guarantees effort and visibility, which you control — not a click number you don't.)*

Why RM199 not RM499 to start: you're trading a lower founding price for **proof and testimonials**. Five paying founding partners + their quotes = the social proof that lets you charge RM499 to partner #6. Don't discount further than this. If they balk, add value (extra placement, a reel feature), don't cut price.

**Scarcity is real here:** only offer "founding partner" to the first 5. After that the price goes up. Say that, and mean it.
