A V60 tip for floral beans: brew a touch hotter (around 92–94°C) and pour slow and steady. Florals and sweetness sit in the first half of the extraction, so a gentle, even pour pulls them out cleanly. If a cup tastes thin or sharp, grind a notch finer next time.
