Hey Carmen — welcome in.

You said you're just starting out, you take it black, and you lean toward roasty, comforting flavours. Honestly, that's a lovely place to begin — roasted, chocolatey beans are the friendliest beans to learn on. They're forgiving, they taste good even if your brew isn't perfect, and they don't punish you for still figuring things out.

So I picked five medium-roast Malaysian beans that lean into that toasty, cocoa-ish depth you asked for — all roaster-direct, all easy to love black. A couple come as small bags so you can try without committing to a kilo, and a couple are a little treat above RM30 because they were too comforting to leave out. I've lined them up easiest to most indulgent.

The comfort classic

A starting-out tip, no gear required: roasted, chocolatey beans like these are the most forgiving coffee there is, so don't stress about doing it "right." A French press or a simple drip is all you need — medium grind, water just off the boil, give it about four minutes, and you're there. As you get comfortable, we'll help you dial it in. For now, just enjoy the cup.
