A V60 tip for light roasts: these beans are dense, so brew hot — 94–96°C — and grind a touch finer than you would for a medium roast. That's what unlocks the sweetness and stops a light, floral cup from tasting thin or sour. Drinking it black, you'll taste every bit of that clarity.
