A V60 tip: brew with water just off the boil (about 92–94°C) and a medium grind. For these nutty-fruity beans, a slightly slower pour pulls out more sweetness — drinking it black, that sweetness is what you'll notice most. If a cup tastes flat, grind a little finer; if it tastes sharp, go a little coarser.
