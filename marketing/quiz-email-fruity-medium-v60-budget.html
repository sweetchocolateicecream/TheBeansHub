Hey Pravind — I found your beans.

So here's the thing: you and I drink coffee the same way. Fruity, black, no milk, brewed slow on a V60. That's my exact setup, so I had a bit of fun digging through the catalogue for you.

Quick honest note — most "fruity" beans at this price are espresso blends built to be softened with milk. You drink it black on a pour-over, so I kept the five that genuinely come alive as filter. They're all roaster-direct and around your budget (most sell in 250g bags, so you'll see a couple just over RM30 a pack), and any of them would make a lovely cup. I've lined them up brightest to softest so you can find your spot on the spectrum.

One V60 tip, friend to friend: keep these a notch coarser and your water just off the boil (around 92°C). Fruity beans get bitter when you over-extract them — back off a little and you'll taste way more of the berry and citrus, way less of the bite. Drinking it black like you do, that difference is everything.
