# Brand Voice — The Beans Hub

## Voice in One Line

Warm, knowledgeable, community-first. Sounds like a friend who's deep into specialty coffee but never makes you feel like an outsider for not knowing something.

---

## Tone Breakdown

| Quality | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | Speaks to people, not at them. Uses "you" naturally. Feels personal, not broadcast. |
| **Community-driven** | References the local scene, celebrates Malaysian roasters by name, acknowledges the reader's taste preferences without assuming expertise. |
| **Knowledgeable** | Has genuine depth on coffee — origins, processing methods, taste notes, brew styles — but wears it lightly. |
| **Inclusive** | Never gatekeeps. Beginners are welcome. Enthusiasm matters more than expertise. |
| **Grounded** | Honest and low-hype. Lets quality speak. Doesn't need to shout. |

---

## What It Should Never Sound Like

- **Corporate / stiff** — no formal headers, no "We are pleased to announce", no buzzword soup
- **Overhyped / salesy** — no "BEST BEANS EVER 🔥🔥🔥", no urgency manufactured from nothing
- **Gatekeeping** — never implies that someone's taste is wrong or that you have to know obscure terms to belong
- **Generic lifestyle** — coffee as an aesthetic prop with no substance ("morning ritual ☕ hustle hard"); real content over vibes-only posts

---

## Vocabulary

**Use freely:**
- taste notes, roast profile, origin, single origin, blend, process (washed, natural, honey)
- local roaster, Malaysian-roasted, roastery
- discover, explore, try, find
- batch, freshly roasted
- light / medium / dark roast
- fruity, floral, chocolatey, nutty, bright, clean, heavy body — flavour-forward language

**Use carefully (not every post):**
- cupping, third wave, specialty grade — valid but can feel insider; explain in context
- terroir — fine in editorial, not in casual social posts

**Avoid:**
- "premium" (overused, means nothing)
- "artisanal" (same)
- "journey" used metaphorically
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Acronyms without spelling out first time (SCA, etc.)

---

## Do / Don't Patterns

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Not sure where to start? Filter by taste note." | "Our platform features a robust taxonomy of sensory descriptors." |
| "This week we're featuring [Roaster] from KL — their natural Ethiopian is something else." | "We are proud to partner with another exceptional local business." |
| "Finished your last bag? Here are 5 beans that taste similar." | "Don't miss out on our amazing selection!" |
| "Chocolatey, low acid, great for pour-over — add it to your shortlist." | "Premium quality beans at competitive prices." |
| "If you've never tried a natural processed bean, this is a great starting point." | "For the true coffee connoisseur only." |

## Example Phrasing by Context

**Discovery post (highlighting a roaster):**
> "Discovered [Roaster Name] recently and couldn't stop thinking about their washed Kenyan. Bright, clean, almost berry-like at the finish. On the platform now if you want to check it out."

**Educational post (beginner-friendly):**
> "Taste notes aren't just marketing. They're a shortcut. If you like dark chocolate and low acidity, filter by those and you'll land on the right roast level 90% of the time."

**Awareness post (new user welcome):**
> "All Malaysian-roasted specialty beans, in one place. Filter by taste, roast level, or roaster. That's it. Go explore."

**Community / local pride:**
> "The Malaysian specialty coffee scene is genuinely impressive and most people outside of it have no idea. We're here to fix that."
