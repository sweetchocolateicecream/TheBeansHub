Hey — thanks for taking the taste quiz. You told us you lean fruity and floral, pull espresso at home, and want to keep it wallet-friendly. Good news: some of the most exciting beans Malaysian roasters carry hit exactly that brief.

Here are three to start with, all under RM35 for 200g, all happy on an espresso machine. They run from light to medium, so you can taste how roast shifts the same kind of bean from delicate and tea-like to rounder and sweeter.

Rose, water apple, peach. About as floral as a cup gets — like sipping a clean, perfumed iced tea. Pulled as espresso it stays bright and aromatic.

Medium roast

Guatemala

Floral and citrus with a touch of chocolate. The crowd-pleaser of the three — a sweeter, fuller shot that still nods to your floral side. And it's the cheapest here.

A small espresso tip: floral, lightly-roasted beans like these taste best a touch cooler and a little finer than a dark roast. If your first shot tastes sharp, grind finer and slow the pour — you'll find the sweetness.
