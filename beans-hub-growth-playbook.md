# The Beans Hub — Growth & Monetisation Playbook
### Framed through Alex Hormozi's principles

---

## What You Actually Have (and Why It's More Valuable Than You Think)

The Beans Hub is not a coffee blog. It is a **demand aggregator** — you have built the place where Malaysian specialty coffee buyers go to discover beans and roasters. That is the asset. Roasters would kill for what you have: a pre-qualified audience of people already searching for specialty coffee.

Hormozi's core insight: **you are sitting on a starving crowd**. Malaysian specialty coffee is growing fast. The buyers are there. The roasters are there. You are the bridge. The bridge charges tolls.

---

## The Value Equation (Apply This to Every Decision)

Hormozi's formula:

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

For **buyers** (coffee drinkers), your dream outcome is: *find the perfect bean without wasting RM on something they hate.*
For **roasters**, their dream outcome is: *get paying customers without spending on ads they don't understand.*

Every feature, offer, and page you build should push that numerator up and the denominator down.

---

## Phase 1 — Build the Audience (Before You Charge Anyone)

Hormozi says: **"The size of your business is determined by the size of the problem you solve, and the size of your audience determines how many people you can solve it for."**

You cannot charge well with thin traffic. So first, manufacture volume.

### DO: Dominate One Channel Completely

Pick **one** platform and post daily for 90 days before touching a second:
- **TikTok / Instagram Reels** is your best bet. Short videos work: "RM15 vs RM60 Ethiopia beans — is it worth it?", "3 KL roasters you've never heard of", "Why your Shopee coffee tastes flat".
- Each video ends with one CTA: "Link in bio → find your bean."
- Do NOT spread yourself across YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and a newsletter at once. You will do all of them badly.

### DO: Build an Email List — Starting Today

Your email list is the only audience you own. Instagram can kill your reach overnight. Your list can't be taken from you.

**Lead magnet to build it:** A one-question quiz — *"What's your coffee personality? Answer 3 questions and we'll match you to your ideal Malaysian roaster."* This costs nothing to build and converts extremely well because it's personalised, instant, and directly tied to what your site already does.

Pop it on every page. Gate the result behind an email.

### DO: Publish Comparison Content Aggressively

You already have good posts (arabica vs robusta, light vs dark roast). Go further. The posts that rank AND convert are:
- "Best Ethiopia beans in Malaysia 2025 — ranked by a non-barista"
- "I tried 12 fermented coffees so you don't have to"
- "Cheapest specialty coffee beans online Malaysia (that are actually good)"

These capture people mid-decision. Mid-decision buyers buy.

### DON'T: Write Educational Content That Doesn't Lead Anywhere

"What is honey process coffee?" is fine as SEO. But if there's no CTA at the bottom pointing to your honey process shop page, you are doing free education for Google's benefit, not yours. Every post must have one next step.

## Phase 2 — The Grand Slam Offer (Make Saying No Feel Stupid)

This is Hormozi's core weapon. A Grand Slam Offer is not a product with a price tag. It is a **bundle of value** so heavy that the price feels like a bargain.

### Your Two Customer Types Require Two Different Offers

---

### OFFER 1: For Roasters — "The Beans Hub Partner Programme"

**The problem you solve for roasters:** They roast great coffee but struggle to reach buyers outside their city. Running Instagram ads is expensive and complicated. Word of mouth is slow.

**The boring version of your offer (what everyone does):**
> "List your roastery on our website for RM199/month."

Nobody wants that. It sounds like a directory listing. No one gets excited.

**The Grand Slam version:**

> **"The Beans Hub Roaster Partner — RM499/month"**
>
> Here's what you get:
> - Your full product catalogue listed and filterable on thebeanshub.com (seen by X,XXX monthly visitors)
> - A dedicated roastery spotlight page (SEO-optimised, ranks for "[your brand] coffee beans Malaysia")
> - 1 featured bean placement on the homepage every month (highest-visibility spot)
> - Monthly traffic report showing exactly how many clicks went to your store
> - Your beans included in our email newsletter (sent to X,XXX subscribers)
> - A "Beans Hub Verified" badge for your own website and Instagram
>
> **Guarantee:** If you don't get at least 50 referral clicks to your store in the first 30 days, we'll extend your listing for free until you do.

The guarantee is the most important part. Hormozi calls this **risk reversal**. You are taking the risk off them and putting it on yourself. This makes "no" feel unreasonable — what are they losing by trying?

**Pricing note:** Do NOT offer a cheap tier to start. Hormozi is explicit: cheap clients are the worst clients. They demand more, complain more, and don't value what you do. Start at a price that filters for serious roasters.

---

### OFFER 2: For Buyers — Build Towards a Subscription

Right now buyers leave your site to purchase from the roaster directly. That's fine for Phase 1. But long-term, Hormozi's model is: **free front end, paid continuity.**

The natural product here is a **monthly coffee subscription box** — curated by The Beans Hub, sourced from your partner roasters.

- RM89/month: 200g from one featured roaster
- RM149/month: 3 x 100g sampler from 3 different roasters

This works because:
1. You already have the roaster relationships
2. Subscribers are your most engaged audience (highest LTV)
3. Roasters love it — guaranteed monthly volume
4. You become the buyer, not just the referrer — your margin is the spread

**Don't build this on Day 1.** Build it once you have 500+ email subscribers and 5+ paying partner roasters. Then launch to the list first.

---

## Phase 3 — Stack Revenue, Don't Replace It

Hormozi teaches: **a business with one revenue stream is fragile.** Here is the stack to build toward, in order:

1. **Roaster Partner Programme** (RM499/month × 10 roasters = RM4,990 MRR) — start here
2. **Featured/sponsored placements** (one-off RM800 for homepage banner, roaster of the month) — upsell existing partners
3. **Email newsletter sponsorships** (once list hits 2,000+, charge RM300–500 per issue)
4. **Subscription box** (once roaster base is stable)
5. **B2B: cafe sourcing service** (cafes pay you to shortlist roasters for them — high ticket, low volume)

Each layer feeds the next. Roasters pay to reach your audience. Your audience grows because you publish content. The content ranks because your roaster partners keep the product catalogue fresh.

## The Most Important DO/DON'T List

### DO

**DO charge more than you're comfortable with.** Hormozi's line: *"Charge so much it hurts a little."* A roaster paying RM499/month will promote the fact they're listed on The Beans Hub. A roaster paying RM49 doesn't care.

**DO make your numbers visible.** "38 roasters. 100+ beans. X,XXX monthly visitors." These numbers are social proof. They make new roasters feel like they're missing out. Put them on your homepage above the fold.

**DO create FOMO for roasters.** "We only onboard 3 new partner roasters per month." This is real if you enforce it. Scarcity makes people act.

**DO follow up.** Hormozi says most sales happen on follow-up 4, 5, 6. If you pitch a roaster and they don't reply, follow up 5 times across 3 weeks. Most people quit after one email. The money is in the follow-up.

**DO document everything as content.** Every roaster visit, every bean you try, every brewing experiment — post it. Hormozi built his entire audience by documenting his business in real time. Do the same for The Beans Hub.

**DO ask for referrals from partner roasters.** Every roaster knows other roasters. After a partner roaster has been live for 30 days, ask: *"Do you know 2 other roasters who'd benefit from this?"* This is free pipeline.

---

### DON'T

**DON'T wait until everything is perfect to charge.** Your site is good enough. Roasters care about one thing: do you send them customers? You send them traffic now, for free. That means you can charge for it today.

**DON'T discount.** If a roaster says RM499 is too much, don't drop to RM299. Instead, ask what outcome they need and stack more value until RM499 feels cheap. Hormozi's rule: *"Never negotiate price. Negotiate value."*

**DON'T serve every roaster.** Some roasters will be high-maintenance, unresponsive, or have poor products. Bad partners slow you down. Hormozi is explicit: fire bad clients. Your time has a cost.

**DON'T try to build an app.** Not yet. You have a working HTML site with SEO traction. Protect that. A React rebuild or "platform upgrade" is a distraction that kills 6 months of growth with zero revenue to show for it.

**DON'T ignore your existing traffic.** You have Google Analytics set up (G-JY42YLKPRM). Log in and find your top 5 landing pages. Those pages are where money is already walking through the door. Optimise CTAs there first before writing any new content.

**DON'T make the buyer do too much work.** Hormozi's value equation: reduce effort. If someone lands on your Ethiopia page and has to click through 4 pages to buy — you lose them. Every product page should have a direct "Buy from roaster →" button above the fold.

## The 90-Day Sprint (Concrete Actions)

**Days 1–30: Foundation**
- Add email capture (quiz or lead magnet) to every page
- Audit your top 5 traffic pages in GA — add a clear CTA to each
- Write your Roaster Partner offer one-pager (one page, plain language, the full value stack + guarantee)
- Make a list of all 38 roasters. Which ones are NOT yet paying partners? That's your pipeline.

**Days 31–60: Revenue**
- Pitch 10 roasters per week via email or DM (personalised, not copy-paste)
- Follow up every non-reply at day 3, day 7, day 14
- Close your first 3 paying partners
- Start posting content 3–5x/week on one platform

**Days 61–90: Scale**
- Use revenue from partners to reinvest in content (photographer, writer, or just more of your own time)
- Ask every partner for a referral
- Launch a waitlist for the subscription box
- Hit RM5,000 MRR before thinking about anything else

---

## The Core Insight to Carry

Hormozi's most repeated point: **the market doesn't care about your effort. It pays for outcomes.**

The Beans Hub's outcome for roasters: *more customers without the pain of running ads.*
The Beans Hub's outcome for buyers: *the right bean found fast, no wasted money.*

Every page, every offer, every email you write — filter it through that. If it doesn't serve one of those two outcomes, cut it.
