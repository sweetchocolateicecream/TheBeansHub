Quick Answers
Frequently asked questions

Can I buy coffee beans directly from The Beans Hub?

No — we're a discovery platform, not a shop. Every "Buy Now" button takes you directly to the roaster's own store so you always buy fresh from the source.

I'm a roaster. How do I get listed?

Fill in the contact form above and select "I'm a Roaster — list my beans". We'll reach out to collect your product details and get you listed.

How often is the catalogue updated?

We add new beans and roasters regularly. Subscribe to our newsletter to get notified when new listings go live.

I found wrong information about a bean. What should I do?

Please let us know using the contact form above. Select "Report wrong/broken info" and describe what you found. We'll fix it quickly.
