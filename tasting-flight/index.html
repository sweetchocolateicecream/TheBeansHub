Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I pay?

Bank transfer. Waitlist members get payment details by email on 30 August; from 31 August, everyone who completes the quiz gets them straight away. Your spot is confirmed when payment is received — first paid, first served. If the 50 fill up before your transfer lands, you get a full refund.

Whole bean or ground?

Your choice — the quiz asks. Whole bean stays fresher; if you don't have a grinder, we'll grind to suit your brewer.

Where do you ship?

West Malaysia only for this batch, and shipping is included in the RM120. We can't reach Sabah, Sarawak or Labuan yet — if an order comes in with an East Malaysia address, we'll refund it in full, so please hold off for now. We'd love to bring a future batch over.

What if I still get a bean I don't like?

Your dealbreakers are excluded outright, and your taste answers drive the picks. But part of the Flight is discovery — expect at least one bean you'd never have picked yourself.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes, while the 50 last — each Flight uses the same quiz profile unless you take the quiz again with a different email.
